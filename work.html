Predicting Search Traffic Decay

FlyRank AI internship. Completed, with a published paper.

Content teams cannot check every page. I built a model that ranks pages by their risk of losing Google search traffic, so editors know what to check first. It reads 90 days of a page's history and predicts if the page will decline in the next 30 days.

asaninvest

Weekly stock forecasts for the Pakistan Stock Exchange

Helps retail investors watch stocks. 4 Random Forest models predict returns 1 to 4 weeks ahead for 24 stocks across 9 sectors (average error 0.048 for 1 week). A FastAPI backend serves them. A daily GitHub Actions job gets new data, retrains and redeploys. I cut the Docker image from 1.59 GB to 837 MB. Now building v2 with more stocks and MLflow tracking.
